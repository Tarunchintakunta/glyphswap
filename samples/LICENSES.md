# Sample licenses

All images in this folder are synthetic, generated by `inference/scripts/make_samples.py` with Pillow. They contain no third-party photos and are released under the repository's MIT license. Text is rendered with OFL-1.1 Google Fonts (Inter, Roboto, Bebas Neue, Montserrat, Playfair Display, Lora, Caveat, Oswald, Open Sans, Noto Sans Devanagari, Noto Sans Telugu); the fonts themselves are downloaded by `make models` and are not committed.

`truth.json` holds the ground-truth text and polygon of every line plus the replacement used by `make eval`.
